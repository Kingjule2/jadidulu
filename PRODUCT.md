# Product Truth: Jadidulu

## Product Identity
- **Product Name:** Jadidulu
- **Tagline:** You Bring the Idea. We Give It Shape.
- **Core Value Proposition:** Jadidulu helps turn early-stage app ideas and business problems into clearer product directions, user journeys, and clickable prototypes you can test and validate before committing to expensive development.
- **Primary Audience:** Founders, product owners, non-technical entrepreneurs, and innovation teams with rough app concepts who want to avoid premature development costs.

## Key Offerings & Capabilities
1. **Web Application Development:** Modern scalable SaaS platforms, dashboards, and responsive web products.
2. **Mobile App Development:** Cross-platform iOS & Android mobile applications.
3. **Desktop App Development:** Productive, high-performance desktop software.
4. **AI Development:** Intelligent workflows, LLM agents, and custom assistants.
5. **Clickable UI/UX Prototypes:** Rapid interactive prototypes tested with real users before coding.

## The 4-Step Process
1. **Tell Us What You're Thinking:** Bring rough ideas, problems, notes, or uncertainties.
2. **Shape the Direction:** Explore user flows, solutions, and key assumptions.
3. **Turn It Into a Prototype:** Convert product direction into an interactive, clickable prototype.
4. **Validate, Then Build:** Gather stakeholder and user feedback, then transition smoothly into production development.

## Operating Principles
- No complete requirements or technical specs required to start.
- Visual prototypes over 100-page specification documents.
- Rapid validation cycles to minimize software engineering risk.
